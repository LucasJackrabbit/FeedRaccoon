## 👋 Welcome to the Den!

Welcome to the official GitHub hub for **FeedRaccoon**! Whether you're here to download the latest release, report a pesky bug, or toss a shiny new feature idea onto the pile, you're in the right place.

FeedRaccoon helps you rummage through the web without algorithms—bringing your favorite blogs, news sites, and feeds straight to your browser toolbar and side panel.

Explore the live **[FeedRaccoon Showcase](https://feedraccoon.pages.dev/)** to see the project in action and preview the latest design and public pages.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **⚡ 1-Minute Live Polling:** Stay up to date with fast background refresh intervals.
* **🔔 Audio Chimes & Notifications:** Custom notification alerts when fresh articles land.
* **📖 Zen Reader Mode:** Clean, distraction-free reading powered by Readability.
* **⚙️ Rule Automations:** Filter, highlight, or organize incoming posts automatically.
* **📂 OPML Import & Export:** Easily migrate your existing subscriptions in seconds.
* **🎨 Adaptive Themes & Side Panel:** Seamless dark/light mode support with both popup and sidebar views.

## 🐛 Reporting Bugs & 💡 Requesting Features

This repository serves as our public tracker for bugs and upcoming improvements. Before opening a new ticket, please take a quick look at the [Issues](../../issues) tab to see if another trash panda has already filed a similar report.

### 🐞 Spotted a Bug?
If something isn't parsing right or an interface element looks broken, **[open a Bug Report](../../issues/new?labels=bug)** and include:
1. **Browser & OS** (e.g., *Firefox 132 on Windows 11*, *Edge on macOS*)
2. **FeedRaccoon Version** (e.g., *v3.2.0*)
3. **Steps to reproduce** the issue
4. **The RSS/Atom Feed URL** (if the bug happens with a specific feed)

### ✨ Have a Shiny Feature Idea?
Got an idea that would make your RSS workflow even better? **[Submit a Feature Request](../../issues/new?labels=enhancement)** describing:
* What the feature does
* How it improves your daily reading experience
